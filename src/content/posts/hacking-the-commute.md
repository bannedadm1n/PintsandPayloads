A commute can be an information goldmine.

People open laptops, display internal URLs, wear company-branded access cards, talk about projects and occasionally leave enough information on a screen for someone nearby to build a useful picture of their organisation.

None of this requires sophisticated tooling.

The interesting part is how little effort is sometimes needed to turn seemingly harmless observations into useful intelligence.

## The lesson

Security awareness tends to focus on obvious attacks. The quieter problem is that normal behaviour leaks information.

Your office does not stop at the front door. If sensitive information is visible on a train, in a coffee shop or at an airport, it is still sensitive information.

That is the sort of thing worth talking about over a pint.
