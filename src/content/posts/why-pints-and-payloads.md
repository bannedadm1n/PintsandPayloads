There are plenty of places to read about cyber security. There are slightly fewer places to read about it without someone trying to sell you something.

So, this is the bit where I write down the things I find interesting.

That might be a red team technique, a particularly stupid application design decision, something learned during a physical engagement, or just a rabbit hole that was too interesting not to document.

## What to expect

Practical security. Real-world tradecraft. Stories where the details can be shared. Occasional opinions.

And probably quite a lot of Active Directory.

The idea is to keep things useful without turning every post into a whitepaper. If it is something worth explaining to someone over a pint, it belongs here.

Cheers.
